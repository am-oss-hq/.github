# Code of conduct

This code applies to every repository in this organization and to every space where its projects are discussed: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and explain your own decisions when you review.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
- Repeatedly pushing a topic after a maintainer has closed it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block contributors who break this code, temporarily or permanently depending on severity.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on [alfredmouelle.com](https://alfredmouelle.com). Reports are handled confidentially.
